AI Case Classifier — Demo

Enter a customer issue. The prototype applies simple keyword rules to demonstrate the workflow. In production, this can be connected to an LLM/API.

How the AI workflow works

  1. Collect case text and Workspace usage signals.
  2. Classify the case into Data, Technical, Workflow, Access or Other.
  3. Estimate priority and identify recurring patterns.
  4. Track status from New → Investigating → Pending Customer → Resolved.
  5. Convert successful resolutions into searchable knowledge/archive tags.
  6. Surface adoption opportunities for proactive Customer Success follow-up.
